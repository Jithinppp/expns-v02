Privacy policy

Last updated · October 19, 2026

The short version

Expns is a personal expense tracker. Your data is yours: nothing is sold, there are no ads, and there is no third-party tracking. This page explains exactly what the app stores and how to erase it.

What the app collects

  • Account details — the name and email address you sign up with.
  • The records you create — accounts, transactions (amounts, dates, notes, merchant names), budgets, categories and recurring rules.
  • Alert history — the budget notifications the app generates for you.

Your password is never stored by the app itself — Supabase authentication keeps it hashed. Expns does not request your location, contacts, camera, microphone or advertising ID.

How it is used

Only to run the app for you: showing your dashboard, syncing your data between your devices, and sending the email links you request (sign-up confirmation and password reset). No profiling, no advertising, no machine learning on your records.

Where it is stored

In your database on Supabase, protected by row-level security — every record is scoped to your signed-in account. All traffic between the app and the service is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

Who can see it

No one else. The app shares no data with third parties. Supabase (database and authentication) and Vercel (this website) process data only to operate the service.

Retention & deletion

Your data stays until you delete your account — Profile → Delete account in the app wipes everything immediately. You can also request deletion by email; both options are on the Delete your account page. Provider backups age out on Supabase's regular backup schedule.

Children

Expns is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly store their data.

This website

Pages on this domain load the Space Grotesk webfont from Google Fonts, and the email-link page loads the Supabase client script from jsDelivr. No cookies, no analytics and no tracking pixels are used.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
