Delete your account

Expns · Android & iOS

In the app — instant

Open Expns and go to:

  • Profile — the settings tab
  • Delete account — just below Sign out
  • Confirm — everything is erased on the spot.

By email

Send a message from your registered address to jithinp.dev@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Expns account”. Requests are processed within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account — name and email address
  • Every account, transaction, budget, category and recurring rule you created
  • Your notification history

This cannot be undone. There is no export and no backup copy you can restore from.

Not ready to leave?

You can simply log out and take a break — your data stays safe and private in the meantime. Deleting the account is the only way to remove the data itself.
